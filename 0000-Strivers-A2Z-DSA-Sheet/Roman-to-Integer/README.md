<h2><a href="https://takeuforward.org/plus/">Roman to Integer</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr><p>Roman numerals are represented by seven different symbols:</p><ul><li>I = 1</li><li>V = 5</li><li>X = 10</li><li>L = 50</li><li>C = 100</li><li>D = 500</li><li>M = 1000</li></ul><p><br></p><p>Roman numerals are typically written from largest to smallest, left to right. However, in specific cases, a smaller numeral placed before a larger one indicates subtraction.</p><p><br></p><p>The following subtractive combinations are valid:</p><ul><li>I before V (5) and X (10) → 4 and 9</li><li>X before L (50) and C (100) → 40 and 90</li><li>C before D (500) and M (1000) → 400 and 900</li></ul><p><br></p><p>Given a Roman numeral, convert it to an integer.</p><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14">Example 1</p><div class="tuf-example text-new_secondary"><p><strong>Input: </strong>s = "III"</p><p><strong>Output: </strong>3</p><p><strong>Explanation: </strong>III = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3</p></div><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14 ">Example 2</p><div class="tuf-example text-new_secondary"><p><strong>Input: </strong>s = "XLII"</p><p><strong>Output: </strong>42</p><p><strong>Explanation: </strong>XL = 40, II = 2 → 40 + 2 = 42</p></div><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14 ">Constraints</p><div class="tuf-dark-content-box"><ul><li>1 &lt;= s.length &lt;= 15</li><li>s contains only the characters ('I', 'V', 'X', 'L', 'C', 'D', 'M')</li><li>It is guaranteed that s is a valid Roman numeral in the range [1, 3999]</li></ul></div><br><br>
