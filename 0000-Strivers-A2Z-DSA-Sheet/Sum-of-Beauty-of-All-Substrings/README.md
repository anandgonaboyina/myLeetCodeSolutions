<h2><a href="https://takeuforward.org/plus/">Sum of Beauty of All Substrings</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr><p>The beauty of a string is defined as the <strong>difference </strong>between the <strong>frequency </strong>of the <strong>most frequent </strong>character and the <strong>least frequent </strong>character <strong>(excluding characters that do not appear) </strong>in that string.</p><p><br></p><p>Given a string s, return the <strong>sum of</strong> <strong>beauty </strong>values of <strong>all possible substrings</strong> of s.</p><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14">Example 1</p><div class="tuf-example text-new_secondary"><p><strong>Input:</strong> s = "xyx"</p><p><strong>Output:</strong> 1</p><p><strong>Explanation:</strong> The substrings with non-zero beauty are:</p><p>- "xyx" → frequencies: x:2, y:1 → beauty = 2 - 1 = 1</p><p>- "xy" → x:1, y:1 → beauty = 0</p><p>- "yx" → y:1, x:1 → beauty = 0</p><p>- "x" or "y" → beauty = 0</p><p>Total sum = 1 (from "xyx") = 1</p></div><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14 ">Example 2</p><div class="tuf-example text-new_secondary"><p><strong>Input:</strong> s = "aabcbaa"</p><p><strong>Output: </strong>17</p><p><strong>Explanation:</strong> Various substrings such as "aabc", "bcba", etc., have non-zero beauty values. Summing all gives 17.</p></div><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14 ">Constraints</p><div class="tuf-dark-content-box"><ul><li>1 &lt;= s.length &lt;= 500</li><li>s consists of only lowercase English letters ('a' to 'z')</li></ul></div><br><br>
