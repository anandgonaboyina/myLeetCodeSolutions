<h2><a href="https://takeuforward.org/plus/">Reverse a LL</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr><p>Given the head of a singly linked list. Reverse the given linked list and return the head of the modified list.</p><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14">Example 1</p><div class="tuf-example text-new_secondary"><p><strong>Input</strong>: head -&gt; 1 -&gt; 2 -&gt; 3 -&gt; 4 -&gt; 5</p><p><strong>Output</strong>: head -&gt; 5 -&gt; 4 -&gt; 3 -&gt; 2 -&gt; 1</p><p><strong>Explanation</strong>: All the links are reversed and the head now points to the last node of the original list.</p></div><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14 ">Example 2</p><div class="tuf-example text-new_secondary"><p><strong>Input</strong>: head -&gt; 6 -&gt; 8</p><p><strong>Output</strong>: head -&gt; 8 -&gt; 6</p><p><strong>Explanation</strong>: All the links are reversed and the head now points to the last node of the original list.</p><p>This can be seen like: 6 &lt;- 8 &lt;- head.</p></div><br><br><p class="tuf-text-14 ">Constraints</p><div class="tuf-dark-content-box"><ul><li>0 &lt;= number of nodes in the Linked List &lt;= 10<sup>5</sup></li><li>0 &lt;= ListNode.val &lt;= 10<sup>4</sup></li></ul></div><br><br>
