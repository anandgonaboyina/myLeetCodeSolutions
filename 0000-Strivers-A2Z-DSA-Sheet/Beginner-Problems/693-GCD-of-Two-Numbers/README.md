<h2><a href="https://takeuforward.org/practice/dsa/gcd-of-two-numbers">693. GCD of Two Numbers</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr>
<div class="ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__richText"><p>You are given two integers <strong>n1</strong> and <strong>n2</strong>. You need find the <strong>Greatest Common Divisor (GCD)</strong> of the two given numbers. Return the <strong>GCD </strong>of the two numbers.</p><p>The <strong>Greatest Common Divisor (GCD)</strong> of two integers is the <strong>largest </strong>positive integer that <strong>divides </strong>both of the integers.</p></div><section class="mt-6"><h3 class="ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__sectionTitle">Example 1:</h3><div class="grid grid-cols-[1rem_1fr] gap-1"><div class="ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__richText"><p><strong>Input</strong>: n1 = 4, n2 = 6</p><p><strong>Output</strong>: 2</p><p><strong>Explanation</strong>: Divisors of n1 = 1, 2, 4, Divisors of n2 = 1, 2, 3, 6</p><p>Greatest Common divisor = 2.</p></div></div></section><section class="mt-6"><h3 class="ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__sectionTitle">Example 2:</h3><div class="grid grid-cols-[1rem_1fr] gap-1"><div class="ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__richText"><p><strong>Input</strong>: n1 = 9, n2 = 8</p><p><strong>Output</strong>: 1</p><p><strong>Explanation</strong>: Divisors of n1 = 1, 3, 9 Divisors of n2 = 1, 2, 4, 8.</p><p>Greatest Common divisor = 1.</p></div></div></section><section class="mt-6"><h3 class="ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__sectionTitle">Constraints:</h3><div class="ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__richText ProblemPanel-module__qBixIa__constraintsBox"><ul><li>1 &lt;= n1, n2 &lt;= 1000</li></ul></div></section>
