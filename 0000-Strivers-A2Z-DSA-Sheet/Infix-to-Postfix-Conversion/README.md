Your task is to convert this expression into its equivalent postfix notation, also known as Reverse Polish Notation (RPN).</p><p><br></p><p>The expression may include:</p><p><br></p><ul><li>Operands: single lowercase English letters (a to z) or single-digit numbers (0 to 9)</li></ul><p><br></p><ul><li>Binary operators: +, -, *, /, ^</li></ul><p><br></p><ul><li>Parentheses: ( and ) to indicate grouping and precedence</li></ul><p><br></p><p>Operator precedence (from highest to lowest):</p><p><br></p><ul><li>^ (exponentiation)</li></ul><p><br></p><ul><li>*, / (multiplication and division)</li></ul><p><br></p><ul><li>+, -
